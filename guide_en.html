FamLedger Official Guide

Welcome to the official FamLedger documentation. This step-by-step guide is designed with absolute simplicity to help you configure and make the absolute most of every single feature in the app.

👋 Chapter 1: Welcome and Setup

FamLedger is built with a strict philosophy: **no central servers, no registration, and 100% offline**. All data you input stays completely locked inside your smartphone's local memory.

Welcome Screen

Upon launching the app for the very first time, you will simply be asked to enter **your Name** (e.g., Antonio) to initialize the main user profile. No email address, password, or registration is required: you are instantly ready to use the app!

📸 SCHERMO_PRIMO_AVVIO_NOME Capture the initial screen where the app greets you and asks to type your name to begin.

Set a Security Password (Optional)

If you wish to secure the application against prying eyes when lending your phone to family or friends, you can enable a **Lock Password**:

  1. Go to Settings (the gear icon in the bottom menu bar).
  2. Scroll down to **Security Password** and toggle it on.
  3. Choose a strong password and enter a clear **Password Hint** (e.g., "My dog's name").

Once enabled, the application will ask for your password every time it opens. The hint will only be shown after 3 consecutive wrong attempts.

Important Security Note!
Since FamLedger does not store anything online, if you set a password and forget it, **no one can recover your data for you**. Always make sure to use a clear hint that only you understand!

🏦 Chapter 2: Account Management (Your Foundation)

Before recording any expense, you must define **where your money is**. In FamLedger, every expense or income must be linked to a specific account.

Create a New Account

  1. Go to the Accounts screen from the bottom menu.
  2. Tap the round "+" button at the bottom right.
  3. Enter the Account Name (e.g., Bank Account, Cash, Revolut Card).
  4. Choose the **Currency** (EUR, USD, etc.).
  5. Set the Initial Balance (how much money is currently in this account).
  6. Pick a **Custom Color** to easily recognize it in charts.
  7. Tap Save.

💸 Chapter 3: Movements and Transactions

Recording daily expenses takes only a few seconds. FamLedger lets you track three types of transactions: **Expenses, Incomes, and Transfers**.

1. Expenses and Incomes

From the main Dashboard, tap the "+" button and fill in the fields:

  • Amount: The sum spent or received.
  • Description: A short label (e.g., "Supermarket").
  • Account: The account to be debited or credited.
  • Category: Select or create a category (e.g., Food, Car, Salary) to catalog your budget.

2. Internal Transfers (Giroconto)

If you transfer money between your own accounts (for example, withdrawing $100 from an ATM to put in your Cash pocket): toggle the **Transfer (Giroconto)** switch at the top of the form. You will be asked for the **Source Account** (where money comes out) and the **Destination Account** (where money goes in).

📸 SCHERMO_AGGIUNGI_MOVIMENTO Capture the Add Transaction form with a standard expense filled in, and one with Transfer active.

3. Recurring Transactions (Bills, Rent, Subscriptions)

If you have fixed monthly costs (e.g., Netflix, Rent, Salary):

  1. Go to Settings > Recurring Transactions.
  2. Tap "+" and set the cycle interval (e.g., Every 1 Month, Every 1 Year).
  3. The app will automatically generate the transaction at the due date, without you having to lift a finger!

📈 Chapter 4: Investments (Markets & Savings) BETA

BETA Version Note
The **Investments** module is currently released in a experimental **Beta** version. We are actively refining online quotation synchronizations and fixed-yield interest calculations. If you notice any discrepancy, please feel free to report it!

FamLedger includes a powerful **Investments** section designed both for stock market investors and for those who prefer steady, fixed-yield savings instruments.

Markets (Variable Portfolio)

Use this section for ETFs, Stocks, or Cryptocurrencies. The app features an online ticker search. Just enter the official **Ticker** symbol (e.g., AAPL or SWDA.MI) and activate the automatic search. The app will fetch the live price and compute your profit or loss based on your **Average Load Price (PMC)** and **Quantity**.

Savings (Fixed Yield)

Use this section for **Certificate of Deposit (CD)**, government bonds, or savings bonds. No ticker is required here: simply set the **Deposited Capital**, the **Annual Interest Rate (%)**, the **Tax Rate** (e.g., 26% or 12.5% for government bonds), and the **Start Date**. The app will calculate the interests accumulated day by day in real-time!

👥 Chapter 5: Shared Expenses (Co-debtors & Settle)

One of the most robust features of FamLedger is handling shared bills (perfect for couples, families, or roommates) to instantly answer: **"Who owes how much to whom?"**.

1. Add Participants

Go to Settings > Participants and add your family members' names (e.g., Mark, Julia).

2. Record a Shared Bill

When entering a expense (e.g., a $100 grocery bill paid entirely by you):

  1. In the transaction form, scroll down to **Co-debtors**.
  2. Select the participants involved (e.g., Mark and Julia).
  3. Choose to split **equally** or assign **custom shares** (e.g., $50 to Mark, $50 to Julia).
  4. Save the expense. The app records that you advanced $100, and Mark and Julia owe you $50 each.
📸 SCHERMO_CO_DEBITORI_FORM Capture the "Co-debtors" section inside the New Transaction form with selected member chips.

3. Quick Settle (Single or Group)

In the main menu, the Debts section gives you a real-time summary. To settle debts, you have two options:

  • Settle Single Person: Tap a debtor's name, choose which account receives the reimbursement, and tap Settle. The app automatically creates a reimbursement transaction, clearing the debt.
  • Settle as a Group (Batch Settle!): If multiple people repay you at once (e.g., at the end of the month), you can select multiple debtors using handy checkboxes and clear all their debts with a single tap.

🎯 Chapter 6: Financial Goals (Free)

**Financial Goals** (Targets) let you plan and track your savings for tangible future projects, like buying a new laptop, a vacation, or building an emergency fund.

Create and Track a Goal

  1. Navigate to the Goals screen from the main menu.
  2. Tap the "+" button at the bottom right.
  3. Enter the **Goal Title** (e.g., New Laptop).
  4. Set the **Target Sum** you wish to accumulate (e.g., $1,200).
  5. Select a **Target Due Date** to complete your saving goal.
  6. Link a **Linked Account** where you stash this specific money.

The application automatically tracks your progress, displaying a **visual percentage bar (%)** and computing the exact monthly saving quota needed from today to the due date to hit your goal stress-free!

🔍 Chapter 8: Anomaly Detection (Free Feature)

The **Anomaly Detection** tool scans your database continuously for potential mistakes or human typing errors to help keep your data perfectly clean.

What anomalies are tracked?

  • Duplicate Expenses: Warns you instantly if you recorded the exact same amount for the same merchant on the same day. Prevents counting a supermarket bill or subscription twice!
  • Unusual Expenses: Automatically flags unusual spending spikes compared to your rolling monthly average in that specific category.

🏷️ 9.1 Custom Categories Management

In FamLedger, you are completely free to build the transaction categories that fit your real life. The app does not lock you into rigid templates!

Create a Category:

  1. In Settings > Categories, tap the "+" button.
  2. Type the **Category Name** (e.g., Restaurants, Gym, Rent).
  3. Pick a **Color** (which will be used harmoniously in the Dashboard).
  4. Select a **Theme Icon** for quick visual scanning of movements.

You can edit or delete categories at any time. All existing transactions associated with them will update automatically!

🔄 9.2 Recurring Transactions & Automations

Don't log the same bills by hand every month! For recurring bills, streaming subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify), rent, or salary, set an automatic rule:

  1. In Settings > Recurring Transactions, tap the "+" button.
  2. Fill in the details (amount, description, account, and category).
  3. Set the **Frequency** (e.g., every 1 Month, every 1 Year, every 2 Weeks).
  4. At the due date, FamLedger will automatically inject the transaction and send you a notification.
